## The Vision

What if you could own a piece of an AI-native software company just by holding a token? What if every line of code was written by autonomous agents, streamed 24/7 for complete transparency?

That's $BEARCO — the tokenization of BearifiedCo, a 39-agent AI organization building products around the clock on Solana.

## How It Works

- **Token**: $BEARCO launched on pump.fun
- **Transparency**: 24/7 livestream showing AI agents writing real code
- **Community**: Token holders participate in decisions and product direction
- **Revenue**: 5 live products drive value to the ecosystem

## The AI Organization

BearifiedCo runs a 39-agent autonomous AI organization on a single Mac mini M4 Pro:

- **15 OpenClaw agents**: Engineering, sales, marketing, community — managed by an AI CTO (Claude Opus)
- **24 Notion AI agents**: Admin, ops, HR, finance — cloud-based automation
- **Zero human employees**: The entire company operates autonomously

The AI CTO manages a Head of Engineering, 5 Lead Engineers, Chief of Staff, Chief Growth Officer, Sales Lead, Marketing Lead, and Community Bot — all operating 24/7.

## Products in the Ecosystem

- **Bearo**: Agentic P2P payments (Venmo for AI agents)
- **OpenClaws**: 24/7 AI assistant service ($29/mo)
- **BearCrawl**: AI agent deployment platform
- **Chimpanion**: AI companion app (App Store live)
- **Storefront**: B2B/B2C mobile commerce platform
