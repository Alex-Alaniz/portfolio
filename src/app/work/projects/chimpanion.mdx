## Overview

Chimpanion is an AI companion app live on the App Store. Built natively in Swift for iOS, it provides real-time market analysis, trading insights, and crypto intelligence — your personal AI trading buddy.

## Key Features

- **Market Analysis**: Real-time crypto market data and trend analysis
- **Trading Insights**: AI-powered signals and pattern recognition
- **Native iOS**: Built in Swift for the best possible mobile experience
- **Always Learning**: Adapts to market conditions and user preferences

## Technical Stack

- **Platform**: Native iOS (Swift, SwiftUI)
- **AI**: Claude-powered analysis and conversation
- **Data**: Real-time market feeds, on-chain analytics
- **Distribution**: Live on the Apple App Store
