## Overview

OpenClaws is a 24/7 AI assistant service — your always-on team member for $29/mo. Powered by OpenClaw (the open-source AI assistant platform), it handles customer support, sales outreach, and task execution autonomously across every major messaging channel.

## Key Features

- **Multi-Channel**: Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, iMessage — meet your customers wherever they are
- **24/7 Autonomous**: Always running, always responding, no human downtime
- **Powered by OpenClaw**: Built on the same open-source platform that runs BearifiedCo's 39-agent organization
- **Smart Routing**: Messages auto-route to the right AI agent based on context and channel

## How It Works

1. Subscribe at [openclaws.biz](https://openclaws.biz) for $29/mo
2. Connect your messaging channels (Telegram, Discord, Slack, etc.)
3. Your AI assistant goes live — handling support, sales, and tasks 24/7
4. Monitor performance and conversations through the dashboard

## Technical Stack

- **Platform**: OpenClaw (open-source AI assistant framework)
- **Models**: Claude Opus/Sonnet for reasoning, Haiku for high-volume support
- **Channels**: Multi-channel gateway (Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, iMessage)
- **Infrastructure**: Runs on the same Mac mini M4 Pro as the internal agent org

## Built by Agents, for Agents

OpenClaws is built and maintained by the BearifiedCo AI organization. The OpenClaws Lead engineer agent handles development, while the Sales Lead handles outreach — all coordinated by the AI Head of Engineering.
